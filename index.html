Snack Lore

Photograph an ingredient list. See what official sources say about each ingredient.

What it does

  • Reads the ingredient list from a photo, or from text you type or paste.
  • Looks up every ingredient in official sources stored in the app: the US, the EU, Great Britain and Canada.
  • Shows what each place says, with the reason and the source. Each place keeps its own result.
  • Names the allergens a label itself states, and the ones you ticked in your profile.
  • Keeps your History, with photos, on every phone you sign in on.

What it does not do

Snack Lore is not a safety check, an allergy test or a certification. "No flags found" means the loaded sources have nothing to flag, nothing more. Always read the printed label yourself, especially for an allergy.
