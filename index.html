United States, European Union, Great Britain and Canada. Each place keeps its own answer.

From photo to answer in three steps

The text is read from your photo on your phone or, with the Online reader, by our reading service; you choose with Change on the home screen. The answers come from a database of regulatory facts that the app keeps up to date, never from a guess.

1

Photograph the list

Scan the label, choose a photo, share one from another app, or type the ingredients. A photo can be cropped to the list first, and you can correct the text that was read before it is checked.

2

Every ingredient is looked up

Names, E numbers and spelling variants are matched to official records for each place you chose. An ingredient that appears twice is checked once.

3

Read each place's answer

Each answer carries its reason. Places are shown side by side, never merged into one.

The screens you will use

Start from Home, crop the photo if you like, read the answer in the popup or on the result screen, and find every scan again in History. The bar at the bottom takes you between Home, History, Scan, People and You; the bell at the top is Notifications. These are screenshots of the app.

What the results mean

Eight states, each with a fixed meaning. The colour is not a verdict; the words and the shape are.

WarningNot allowed in food in that place.
Not allowed from a set dateStill allowed today. A rule changes on a set date, which the result shows.
CautionUnder review. This is not a ban.
Limited useAllowed, with conditions.
No warnings foundNothing in the rules we hold restricts it. Absence of a warning is not a safety finding.
Couldn't verifyWe could not decide. A misread word is the usual cause. Fix it and check again.
Named as a groupA class word such as flavours, so there is nothing specific to check.
Plain foodAn ordinary food, not an additive.

Allergens on this label

The allergens printed on the label, in the ingredients or in a "Contains" or "May contain" line, are named for the US and the EU. Tick yours in My allergens, or ask for them right after you log in, and the rows that carry them are marked. It reports what the label says; it never says an allergen is absent.

It keeps working when you leave

A scan, a check, a sync or a share carries on when you minimise the app, lock the phone or close the app, and the phone tells you when a scan is ready. If you scan with the Online reader and there is no internet, the first reading is kept and the online one finishes by itself when a connection returns; you get a notification then.

Notifications, in the app and on the phone

The bell counts what you have not seen: a scan that finished while you were away, new ingredient data, and saved scans whose ingredients changed in the sources. Filter by All, Unread and Read, or tap Mark all as read. You can switch the phone notifications off in Settings.

Your History, on every phone

Scans are saved with their photo and place. Sign in with your email or with Google on a new phone and your History, allergens and settings are there. Removing something only takes it out of your view; hold a scan in History to select several. Scans made without a connection are sent when it returns, also when the app is closed.

Built on public regulatory data

The answers come from official regulatory records that the app keeps up to date, and each answer comes with its reason. Nothing is guessed.

What Snack Lore does not do. It is not a safety check, an allergy test or a certification, and it says nothing about religious or dietary status. "No warnings found" means the loaded sources have nothing that restricts it, nothing more. Always read the printed label yourself, especially for an allergy.
