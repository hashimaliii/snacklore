From photo to answer in three steps

The text is read from your photo on your phone or, with the Online reader, by our reading service; you choose on the home screen. The answers come from a database of regulatory facts that the app keeps up to date, never from a guess.

1

Photograph the list

Scan the label, choose a photo, or type the ingredients. You can correct the text that was read before it is checked.

2

Every ingredient is looked up

Names, E numbers and spelling variants are matched to official records for each place you chose. An ingredient that appears twice is checked once.

3

Read each place's answer

Each answer carries its reason. Places are shown side by side, never merged into one.

The screens you will use

Start from Home, read the answer on the result screen, and find every scan again in History. Each screen is drawn here with the app's own wording.

What the results mean

Eight states, each with a fixed meaning. The colour is not a verdict; the words and the shape are.

FlaggedNot allowed in food in that place.
Flagged (future)Still allowed today. A rule changes on a set date.
AdvisoryUnder review. This is not a ban.
Limited useAllowed, with conditions.
No flags foundNothing to flag in the rules we hold. Absence of a flag is not a safety finding.
Couldn't verifyWe could not decide. A misread word is the usual cause. Fix it and check again.
Named as a groupA class word such as flavours, so there is nothing specific to check.
Plain foodAn ordinary food, not an additive.

Allergens the label names

The allergens printed on the label, in the ingredients or in a "Contains" or "May contain" line, are named for the US and the EU. Tick yours in your profile and the rows that carry them are marked. It reports what the label says; it never says an allergen is absent.

Your History, on every phone

Scans are saved with their photo and place. Sign in with your email or with Google on a new phone and your History, allergens and settings are there. Removing something only takes it out of your view; hold a scan in History to select several. Scans made without a connection are sent when it returns.

Built on public regulatory data

The answers come from official regulatory records that the app keeps up to date, and each answer comes with its reason. Nothing is guessed.

What Snack Lore does not do. It is not a safety check, an allergy test or a certification, and it says nothing about religious or dietary status. "No flags found" means the loaded sources have nothing to flag, nothing more. Always read the printed label yourself, especially for an allergy.
