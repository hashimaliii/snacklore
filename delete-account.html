Delete your Snack Lore account

You can ask us to delete your Snack Lore account and the personal data linked to it. There is no button in the app for this: you ask by email, and we do it.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to snacklore.app@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Snack Lore account. Send it from the email address you signed up with. If you use "Continue with Google", send it from your Google account's address.
  2. We reply to that address to confirm it is you.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days of your confirmation, and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and password, and your profile (name, phone number, date of birth, country, gender and picture, whichever you gave).
  • Your allergens and settings.
  • Your saved scans: the text, the results, the photos, the places and the names you gave them, including the ones you removed from History and the ones older than the newest 100.
  • Your activity log, the reports you sent, the shared result links you made (they stop working) and the corrections you made.
  • Your place on any household list. A list you started that still has other members stays for them; its people are theirs.

What may remain

  • Copies in our database backups, until those backups expire.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep.
  • What other people on a household list saw or noted while you were on it.

In the app

Removing a scan, deleting all history or removing a picture in the app only takes it out of your view. Asking here is what removes it from our records. See the privacy policy for everything we keep and why.
