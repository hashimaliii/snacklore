Snack Lore privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026. Snack Lore reads a food ingredient list from a photo or typed text and shows what official sources say about each ingredient. This page says what the app stores, where, and how long it is kept.

Contact for privacy questions: snacklore.app@gmail.com

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Email address and passwordTo create and open your accountOur hosting provider, Supabase. The password is stored as a hash, never as text. If you choose "Continue with Google" instead, Google tells the app your email address, name, picture and language; we never see your Google password.
Name, first and last name, phone number, date of birth, country, profile picture, allergens you tick (all optional)To personalise the app and mark ingredients on your listYour account at Supabase, so a new phone shows the same name, allergens and picture. Activity log: screens opened, settings changed, scans with counts and seconds, errorsTo keep the app working and improve itSupabase, linked to your account and a random device id. It never contains the photo, the ingredient text or a place.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the account system, the ingredient database and the server functions.
  • OpenRouter and the provider of the Qwen vision model process a photo when the Online reader is used, which is the default; choose "On device" on the home screen to avoid it. Their own terms apply to what they do with it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

How long your data is kept

  • Your account details, activity log and reports are kept for as long as we run the service. They are not removed when you stop using the app.
  • A scan that is removed from History (one at a time, or with Delete all) is copied to our servers first: its text, results, places and photo are kept.
  • Anything that is removed or replaced later is kept in a restricted archive that the app cannot read and that only we can open.
  • Removing something in the app (a scan, all history, a picture, an allergen) only takes it out of your view. It stays in our database, as described above.
  • Your phone keeps your History and your photos until you remove them or uninstall the app. The copies in your account stay as described above, and appear again if you sign in on a new phone.

Security

Connections to our servers use HTTPS. The app is not a place to store anything you would not want stored.

Children

Snack Lore is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not advice

Snack Lore is not medical, allergy, legal or religious advice and not a safety check. Always read the printed label yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
